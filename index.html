How the world has moved on poverty

A short timeline of milestones behind SDG 1 — and how far there still is to go.

1990

1.9 billion in extreme poverty

Nearly 36% of the world's population lived below the extreme poverty line.

2015

UN adopts the SDGs

193 countries commit to 17 Sustainable Development Goals, with SDG 1 aiming to end poverty in all forms by 2030.

2020

First rise in decades

The pandemic pushed extreme poverty up for the first time in over 20 years, adding roughly 70 million people.

2026

Local action, global goal

Platforms like Saathi aim to close the last mile — connecting individual support directly to people who need it.
